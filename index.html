Every scan pre-registers its top picks here. Verify replays each item’s own 5-minute history and asks whether your buy and sell prices were actually reachable. Realized ROI is a ceiling — it assumes you were first in queue at both ends. The gap between predicted and realized is your slippage: feed it back into Config.

Journal every scan’s top picks

Slippage 0 assumes fills at last-traded prices — optimistic. Journal for a few days, hit Verify, then set slippage to about half of (predicted − realized) ROI. Participation caps how much hourly volume you assume you can capture; 10% is a guess until your fills say otherwise.

Data: OSRS Wiki / RuneLite real-time prices API. Browsers can’t send the custom User-Agent the wiki asks of automated tools, so keep usage reasonable — this app fetches 4 endpoints per scan and caches the item mapping for 24h. Tax model: 2% of sale (since 29 May 2025), floored, capped 5m/item, bonds & starter tools exempt. Not affiliated with Jagex.
