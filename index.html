Slot allocation

Run a scan first — the allocator spreads your bankroll across your GE slots.

GP/h is what an item earns. %/h is what it earns per gp you tie up. They rank completely differently, and the one that matters is whichever resource you run out of first — the note above says which. Density-greedy with a swap local search: it will trade a small high-ROI pick out of a slot when a bigger absorber earns more gp/hour for the bankroll overall. Idle gp is shown so you can tell when the market, not your bank, is the constraint.

A different trade from the Scan tab.
Scan captures the bid-ask spread and treats volatility as risk. This treats volatility as the point: buy an item trading below its own 30-day average, sell the recovery. Checks z-score, RSI and trend slope on ~30 days of history — and refuses anything in a structural decline, because a nerfed item is cheap for a reason.

Target is the median of the recent range, not the high — reverting to the middle is a far safer assumption than calling the top. Expected profit is already net of the 2% tax. These are holds, not four-hour cycles: there is no guarantee of when, or whether, a price reverts. Downside is the drop to the window's low — not a stop-loss, just the fact that this item has traded that low recently. A reward-to-risk under 1 means the plausible loss exceeds the expected gain; those are filtered out.

Open positions

Nothing held.
Tap a scan row and press Log buy once an offer fills. That starts the item's 4-hour limit timer and stops the scanner recommending what you can no longer buy.

Buy limits

No limits in use.

The window opens on your first buy and resets exactly 4h later, no matter when the rest fills — so buy as much as you can early. Staggering items across the session gives you a rolling reset every 30-60 minutes instead of all eight at once. Dose variants marked ◈ draw from a single shared allowance — buying Prayer potion(4) eats into what you can buy of Prayer potion(1). Selling is never limited, only buying.

Predicted vs realized

observations0

Every scan pre-registers its top picks here. Verify replays each item’s own 5-minute history and asks whether your buy and sell prices were actually reachable. Realized ROI is a ceiling — it assumes you were first in queue at both ends. The gap between predicted and realized is your slippage: feed it back into Config.

Data: OSRS Wiki / RuneLite real-time prices API.
